Questions, answered

Before you download.

What can I do with Hi PG?

Renters can discover PG properties, review availability and pricing, manage bookings and follow fee or support activity. PG owners get tools for properties, rooms, beds, residents, finances, documents and support.

Is the app available for Android?

Yes. The direct APK on this page supports {{APP_MIN_ANDROID}}. An iOS build is not currently offered from this website.

Why does Android ask me to allow installation?

Android shows this confirmation for apps downloaded through a browser. Confirm only when the file came from this official Hi PG website, then disable the browser's installation permission afterwards if you prefer.

Where can I get help?

Use in-app support for property-related requests, or visit our contact and grievance page for account, privacy or general support.

Can I delete my Hi PG account?

Yes. Hi PG includes account deletion inside the app, and an external request process is available on the account deletion page.
